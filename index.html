Facts About Siquijor

Every wave brings a new adventure, every sunset a new dream.

  • "Mystical Island"

    Reputation

    Siquijor is famous for its folklore and mysticism, often called the "Island of Fire" due to its history of healing rituals and traditional witchcraft.

  • Top Diving and

    Snorkeling Spot

    The island boasts stunning marine biodiversity, with popular dive sites like Tulapos Marine Sanctuary and Apo Island nearby.

  • Eco-Tourism and

    Sustainable Efforts

    Siquijor actively promotes sustainable tourism, preserving its natural wonders like Cambugahay Falls and century-old Balete Tree while protecting marine sanctuaries.

  • Home to the Oldest Convent

    in the Philippines

    St. Isidore de Labrador Church and Convent in Lazi, built in the late 1800s, is considered the largest and one of the oldest convents in the country.

Cambugahay Falls

Siquijor BABY!!!

Cambugahay Falls are stunning lagoon-style tiered falls with beautiful turquoise water, tucked away in a tropical valley. You can easily spend a few hours there and if you're into swinging off ropes and jumping into the water, then this will be a true paradise for you.

Paliton Beach

Siquijor YEAH!!!

Paliton Beach, often dubbed "Little Boracay," is famed for its powdery white sand and crystal-clear waters. Situated on the western coast of Siquijor, Paliton is a favourite among photographers and beach lovers seeking postcard-worthy scenery.

Tubod Beach

Siquijor Beach

Tubod Beach is home to Tubod Marine Sanctuary, a designated area of coral and sea on Siquijor island aimed at preserving and promoting marine life. The snorkelling at Tubod Marine Sanctuary is what sets this beach apart from the rest. If you don’t have your own snorkel then no worries, you can hire a set from Coco Grove Beach Resort and head into the sea. It is not far out to the marine sanctuary, depending on whether it is high or low tide you have around 50 metres of seagrass from the beach and after that, you have corals and reefs to explore.

  • Category: Beach

Salagdoong Beach

...

Salagdoong Beach is a stunning beach located in the island of Siquijor in the Philippines. It is known for its crystal-clear waters and powdery white sand. The beach is surrounded by lush green trees and is perfect for swimming, sunbathing, and snorkeling. Salagdoong Beach is also known for its beautiful rock formations that are perfect for cliff diving. In fact, Salagdoong Beach is the only beach in Siquijor Island that has a diving board. The beach is also known for its rich marine life, making it a popular spot for diving enthusiasts.

  • Category: Beach

Tulapos Marine Sanctuary

-

The Tulapos Marine Sanctuary is a small marine reserve located in the north of the island of Siquijor. Established in 1986, it is the oldest marine protected area on the island. It includes mangroves and coral reefs that are home to schools of barracuda, giant clams, starfish, turtles, and several species of clownfish.
